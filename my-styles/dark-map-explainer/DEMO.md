# Demo — 盐城，是从海里长出来的 (Yancheng, grown from the sea; 143.9 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

- **Story** (18 verified lines, typed, no voice): Yancheng has no mountains → a thousand years ago most of it was under the Yellow Sea → it grew out of the sea → salt boiling, 盐渎 → 盐城 → Fan Zhongyan's sea dike (范公堤) → 1128 the Yellow River turns south into the Huai → 700 years of loess silt → the coast pushed east ring by ring → the dike now far inland → reed marshes and water network → Shi Nai'an (legend) → New Fourth Army HQ 1941 → 582 km coast today → Yellow Sea wetlands World Heritage 2019 → birds, cranes, Père David's deer → >10 GW of wind → "still growing into the sea".
- **Shots**: 19 beats, 9 camera moves in total (all 3.6–5.2 s, sine-eased, zoom about a fixed point), the rest locked. Peak rates measured by `scripts/camrate.ts`. Opening frame = the whole city with the "17,700 km², not one mountain" card; ending returns to it with a dashed new ring pushing seaward (示意). Styleframe = 60 s (rings advancing, "约1.2万 平方公里" card, timeline cursor at 1855).
- **Palette**: as STYLE.md §3; land slightly lighter than the reference (user preference).
- **Score**: the user's 老配乐, looped with 4 s crossfades, constant level, 4.4 s fade-out; code SFX (typing ticks, underline whooshes, card bells, scroll whoosh, river riser, sediment hiss, ring pulses, bird chirps). Master −14 LUFS / TP ≤ −1.2 dBTP.
- **Draws what**: `src/Main.tsx` (the whole film: map layers, rings, river, sediment particles, cards, scroll, timeline, ruler, wetlands tidal trees, birds, turbines, corner note), `src/Text.tsx` (typed top-left text + underlines), `src/timeline.ts` (the 18 lines → beat times by reading speed), `src/geo.ts` (projection, ring geometry, camera keys `MOVES`, `camAt`), `src/Art.tsx` (salt-boiling scene, study desk, crane, deer, turbine silhouettes), `src/Cover.tsx` (3:4 / 4:3 covers), `scripts/make_audio.py` (score + SFX + master), `scripts/prep.py` (raw OSM/NE → `public/map.json`, kept for reference; its raw inputs aren't shipped).
- **Build**: `bash demo/build.sh` (low-res by default; `SCALE=1` for 1080p; `FRAMES=a-b` for a quick check; `MUSIC=` your score; `NODE_MODULES=` to reuse an install). Writes to `$OUT`, never into `demo/`.
- **Known flaws**: at ≈ 113 s the "2019" card half covers the 黄海湿地 label; the scroll quote is cited via a paper, not checked against the original gazetteer; the covers carry a small "代码生成" badge (the film itself has none).
- This was the second take on the topic: a 3D white-model version was dropped as slow and dizzy, which is why §6 is strict.
